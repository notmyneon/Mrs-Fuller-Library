NEW CLASS / SCHOOL YEAR

Clear student roster

Removes all students from the active class without deleting the reading data connected to them.

  • Books stay in the library.
  • Book ratings are preserved.
  • Finished / Didn't Finish data is preserved.
  • Past checkout history is preserved.
  • Students disappear from the active roster and library-card page.
Requires two confirmations. This archives students rather than destroying their history.
DANGER ZONE

Reset all library data

Permanently deletes every book, student, checkout, return, rating, and reading-history record in your cloud database.

This action requires two separate confirmations.
